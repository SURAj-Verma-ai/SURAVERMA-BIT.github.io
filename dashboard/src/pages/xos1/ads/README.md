# XOS1 ad scenes

Two full-screen 3D scenes kept from the welcome trials on the XOS1 page, for
use as ads or recordings. Open them on the XOS1 page with `?ad=city` or
`?ad=spatial`; they are not linked from anywhere.

- **City**: a city at night. "Not everyone can use a computer. Everyone can
  talk." One window lights, then the whole city. Planned to grow into the
  impact page, with the whole world lighting up instead of one city.
- **Spatial**: everything a computer does, as things in a space, flown through.
